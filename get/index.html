Why we built it

Residency leaves you little time to study. The recall folders take most of it.

Clinic, on-calls, then a folder of recall files: the same question five times under five names, some with no key, some with two. You sort before you can study.

We did the sorting. Repeats were merged into one question that remembers how often it was seen. Questions with no key, or a key nobody could settle, were left out. What remains is filed under the 12 domains of the blueprint.

Questions

Before you start

Which exams does it prepare me for?

The three written exams of the Saudi Board in family medicine: the Promotion exam, Part One and the Final written exam. It does not cover the clinical exam.

Where do the questions come from?

Board-style review questions, and questions residents reported after past papers. Where a question was reported from an exam, it shows which exam, which years and how many times.

Can I trust the answer keys?

Questions with no key, or with a key that could not be settled, were left out of the bank. Every question but a few dozen carries an explanation. If you disagree with one, the search button under it lets you check the guideline in a tap.

I am R1. Is the bank too much for me?

The bank is the same for every year, because the blueprint is. Your year changes what comes first and which paper you are timed against, and what you cover now stays with you until the Final.

Do I have to install it?

No. You can start in the browser right away, on iPhone or Android. Installed on your home screen it opens full screen and keeps working offline after the first time you open it.

Do I pay anything to try it?

No. You create an account and answer free questions first. You only pay if you decide to open more of the bank.

Is it an official SCFHS product?

No. It is an independent study tool, not affiliated with the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties.
